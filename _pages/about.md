I am a student in the Master's degree in Applied Economics and Markets at the University of Bologna. My interests span a wide range within economics, though most of my recent research has focused on macroeconometrics, macroeconomics, and political economy. Beyond that, I have a strong interest in monetary economics, policy analysis, and geopolitics.

In recent years, I spent a semester on exchange at the University of Sydney and co-founded RATIO Think Tank, where, together with some of my peers, we conduct policy analysis on major and ongoing issues facing Italy, the country most of us come from.
